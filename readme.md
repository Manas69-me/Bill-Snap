This is Bill Snap, an AI-powered bill scanning and expense tracking application.

Users can capture a bill using their phone camera or upload an existing image from their gallery.

The application uses Gemini AI to identify whether the uploaded image is a valid bill and extract important details such as the merchant name, date, total amount and expense category.

If any information is uncertain or incorrect, users can edit it before saving.

Once saved, bills are displayed in a simple list, making it easy to track expenses.

The application also rejects non-bill images, such as selfies and unrelated pictures.

That's Bill Snap — a simple way to scan bills and manage expenses!
